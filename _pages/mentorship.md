Besides my own research, I regularly help my advisors by serving as primary mentors for undergraduate and graduate students in the lab I am associated in. Following are the list of students:

<li> <b>Yu Komazawa</b> (<i>Current Senior undergraduate student, School of Fisheries Sciences at Hokkaido University</i>): Yu is a visiting student through Uehiro Intructor-Led Research Experience between UH and Hokkaido University. During his visit in Seo lab, Yu worked on understanding the influence of air-sea fluxes and water clarity on upper ocean mixing at OWS Papa using General Ocean Turbulence Model (GOTM). Explore his <a href="https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/oceanography/inbound-ilre-course-updates">journey</a> or dive into the <a href="https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/oceanography/ucao-welcomes-hokkaido-university-students-for-ilre">program details</a>. </li>

<li> <b>Debarshi Sarkar</b> (<i>Current PhD Student, Engineering and Applied Sciences, UMassD</i>): Debarshi is currently working on extending Nick's undergraduate thesis to incorporate observations from multiple OMNI moorings in the Arabian Sea and multiple reanalysis products for his first chapter of PhD. The goal behind this chapter is to report biases in measured and derived air-sea interaction parameters, as to address one likely issue of faulty monsoon forecasting. This work has been presented at Ocean Sciences Meeting 2024 and some other International and local conferences. Sid is serving as a colleague mentor for this work.</li>

<li> <b>Ersen'S Joseph</b> (<i>BS, Mechanical Engineering '22, UMassD</i>): Ersen'S worked with us as a part of Undergraduate Research Internship Program, UMassD (from January-December 2021) as well as UMassD-WHOI-Blue Economy Internship (from January-May 2022). His project involved analyzing RAMA mooring data in the Bay of Bengal, developing a numerical particle tracking model and comparing with observed particle motion in open ocean as well as using powerful computation resources to analyze remote sensing datasets in order to uncover the seasonality in the Bay of Bengal. Ersen'S has presented this work at many local conferences like MassURC, IMS symposium, UMassD Sigma-Xi Research exhibit etc. Ersen'S is currently an Engineer in Upper Ocean Processes lab in WHOI after completing his masters at UMassD with Drs.
